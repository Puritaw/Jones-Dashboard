🌲 Forest Conservation Advisory

🔔 What You Should Do:

  • Respect wildlife – Avoid disturbing animals and observe them from a distance.
  • Stay on designated trails – Prevent soil erosion and protect delicate plant life.
  • Do not litter – Carry your trash out with you and dispose of it responsibly.
  • Avoid starting fires – Even in healthy forests, fire risks can still exist.
  • Educate others – Share knowledge about the importance of preserving pristine forests.

🌳 Why It Matters:

  • Carbon capture and climate regulation
  • Clean air and water
  • Supporting biodiversity
  • Sustaining local communities

Let’s work together to protect this natural treasure for future generations. 🌎💚

Red Zone

ข้อมูลและรายละเอียดของพื้นที่สีแดง

🔥 Wildfire Risk Alert

Please take the following precautions to protect yourself and your community:

  • Avoid outdoor burning of any kind, including agricultural waste or trash.
  • Stay alert for signs of smoke or fire in nearby forest areas.
  • Report any signs of wildfire immediately to local authorities or call the national emergency hotline (e.g., 191).
  • Protect your health – If smoke is present, stay indoors with windows and doors closed.
  • Prepare an emergency kit with essentials in case evacuation becomes necessary.
  • Stay updated through local news, weather apps, or official announcements from authorities.

Together, we can reduce the risk and impact of wildfires in our community.

Stay safe.
